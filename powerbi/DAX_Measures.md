# Power BI DAX Measures & Data Model Documentation

This document defines all DAX measures implemented in the **Retail Stores Sales Analysis (2023–2024)** Power BI Data Model. All calculations have been cross-validated against Python (Pandas) and MySQL query results to guarantee mathematical accuracy.

---

## 1. Core Financial Measures (Base Aggregations)

### `[Total Revenue]`
```dax
Total Revenue = SUM(sales[revenue])
```
- **Description:** Sum of net sales revenue after discounts across all transactions.
- **Format:** Currency (`$#,##0.00` or `$#,##0,,.00M`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **$24,498,421.43**

### `[Total Cost]`
```dax
Total Cost = SUM(sales[cost])
```
- **Description:** Total cost of goods sold (COGS).
- **Format:** Currency (`$#,##0.00`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **$14,806,220.22**

### `[Total Profit]`
```dax
Total Profit = SUM(sales[profit])
```
- **Description:** Net operating profit generated across transactions (`revenue - cost`).
- **Format:** Currency (`$#,##0.00` or `$#,##0,,.00M`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **$9,692,201.21**

### `[Total Quantity]`
```dax
Total Quantity = SUM(sales[quantity])
```
- **Description:** Total volume of chocolate units sold.
- **Format:** Whole Number (`#,##0`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **2,999,021 units**

### `[Total Orders]`
```dax
Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT(sales[order_id])
```
- **Description:** Total number of distinct sales orders recorded.
- **Format:** Whole Number (`#,##0`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **1,000,000 orders**

## 2. Derived Performance & Efficiency Measures

### `[Profit Margin %]`
```dax
Profit Margin % = 
DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Revenue], 0)
```
- **Description:** Gross operating margin as a percentage of revenue.
- **Format:** Percentage (`0.00%`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **39.56%**

### `[Average Order Value (AOV)]`
```dax
Average Order Value = 
DIVIDE([Total Revenue], [Total Orders], 0)
```
- **Description:** Average transaction value per order basket.
- **Format:** Currency (`$#,##0.00`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **$24.50**

### `[Average Selling Price (ASP)]`
```dax
Average Selling Price = 
DIVIDE([Total Revenue], [Total Quantity], 0)
```
- **Description:** Effective net price realized per chocolate unit.
- **Format:** Currency (`$#,##0.00`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **$8.17**

### `[Average Discount %]`
```dax
Average Discount % = 
AVERAGE(sales[discount])
```
- **Description:** Mean promotional discount percentage applied across transactions.
- **Format:** Percentage (`0.00%`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **9.99%** (~10.00%)

## 3. Time Intelligence & Year-over-Year (YoY) Measures

### `[2023 Revenue]`
```dax
2023 Revenue = 
CALCULATE(
    [Total Revenue],
    calendar[year] = 2023
)
```
- **Format:** Currency (`$#,##0.00`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **$12,246,904.53**

### `[2024 Revenue]`
```dax
2024 Revenue = 
CALCULATE(
    [Total Revenue],
    calendar[year] = 2024
)
```
- **Format:** Currency (`$#,##0.00`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **$12,251,516.90**

### `[YoY Revenue Growth $]`
```dax
YoY Revenue Growth $ = 
[2024 Revenue] - [2023 Revenue]
```
- **Format:** Currency (`+$#,##0.00;-$#,##0.00`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **+$4,612.37**

### `[YoY Revenue Growth %]`
```dax
YoY Revenue Growth % = 
DIVIDE([YoY Revenue Growth $], [2023 Revenue], 0)
```
- **Format:** Percentage (`+0.00%;-0.00%`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **+0.04%**

## 4. Entity-Level Productivity Measures

### `[Revenue per Store]`
```dax
Revenue per Store = 
DIVIDE([Total Revenue], DISTINCTCOUNT(stores[store_id]), 0)
```
- **Format:** Currency (`$#,##0.00`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **$244,984.21**

### `[Profit per Store]`
```dax
Profit per Store = 
DIVIDE([Total Profit], DISTINCTCOUNT(stores[store_id]), 0)
```
- **Format:** Currency (`$#,##0.00`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **$96,922.01**

### `[Revenue per Customer]`
```dax
Revenue per Customer = 
DIVIDE([Total Revenue], DISTINCTCOUNT(customers[customer_id]), 0)
```
- **Format:** Currency (`$#,##0.00`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **$489.97**

### `[Orders per Customer]`
```dax
Orders per Customer = 
DIVIDE([Total Orders], DISTINCTCOUNT(customers[customer_id]), 0)
```
- **Format:** Decimal Number (`0.00`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **20.00 orders**

## 5. Data Quality & Exception Handling Measures

### `[Orphan Product Revenue]`
```dax
Orphan Product Revenue = 
CALCULATE(
    [Total Revenue],
    sales[product_id] IN { "P0000", "P0201" }
)
```
- **Description:** Revenue generated by transaction records referencing non-catalog product keys.
- **Format:** Currency (`$#,##0.00`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **$238,623.66** (0.97% of total revenue)

### `[Catalog Product Revenue]`
```dax
Catalog Product Revenue = 
CALCULATE(
    [Total Revenue],
    NOT(sales[product_id] IN { "P0000", "P0201" })
)
```
- **Format:** Currency (`$#,##0.00`)
- **Validated Benchmark:** **$24,259,797.77** (99.03% of total revenue)
